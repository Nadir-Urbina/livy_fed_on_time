Deliberately shares no words with the title. Apple indexes both fields, so
repeating "feeding" or "tracker" here would spend characters on keywords you
already rank for. This adds *bottle*, *shared*, *log*, and *parents*.

## Description

```
It's 3am. Did she already eat?

Livy is one shared feeding log for everyone who looks after your baby — so
nobody guesses, nobody double-feeds, and nobody has to whisper "did you
already do it?" across a dark room.

Every feed shows who logged it. Partners, grandparents, night nurses, and
sitters all see the same record on their own phones, updated the moment
someone taps.

FORMULA RECALL ALERTS
Livy watches the FDA's recall feed and checks it against the formula brands
you've actually logged — not a generic list. If a recall touches your brand,
you'll know, with the lot codes laid out plainly.

BUILT FOR THE MIDDLE OF THE NIGHT
The home screen is a glowing nightlight dial that fills as the next feed
approaches — readable at a glance, one-handed, without waking anyone. Dark
mode isn't an option here; it's the default.

ONE TAP TO LOG
Amount, time, and who fed. Add a brand, a note, or how it went if you want to
— skip it entirely if you don't. Solid meals get their own log once you start
purées and finger food.

EVERYONE STAYS IN SYNC
· Invite caregivers with a short code
· Reminders land on every connected device
· Schedule changes are proposed and approved, so nobody silently moves feeds
· See who's been carrying the night shift

STREAKS, BADGES, AND SMALL WINS
Livy notices on-time streaks and clean handoffs, and says so. It's a small
thing at 4am, and it turns out to matter.

TRENDS WORTH SEEING
Feeds per day, average amounts, and how intervals are drifting as your baby
grows — so you can tell your pediatrician what's actually been happening.

A NOTEBOOK, NOT A CLINICIAN
Livy keeps records; she doesn't give medical advice. Where the app repeats
general guidance — when solids usually start, how intervals tend to change —
every statement links to its source at the CDC, the AAP, or the FDA. Your
pediatrician is the one who knows your baby.

PRIVATE, AND AD-FREE
No ads, ever. Your household's data belongs to your household.

Livy is a paid app with a 7-day free trial. One subscription covers the whole
household — invited caregivers never pay.
```
