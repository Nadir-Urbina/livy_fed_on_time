Privacy Policy

Livy — Fed On Time · HizWayz, LLC · Effective August 2, 2026

Livy — Fed On Time ("Livy", "the app") helps caregivers in one household share a record of a baby's formula feeds. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Account information — your email address and the first name you choose to display, provided through Sign in with Apple or email sign-in.
  • Baby profile — the name and birth date you enter for your baby.
  • Feeding records — feed times, amounts, formula brands, and any symptom notes (for example gas, spit-up, or fussiness) that you choose to log. Symptom notes may be considered health-related information; they are entered entirely at your discretion.
  • Household data — caregiver names, invite codes, streaks, and badges shared within your household.
  • Device tokens — a push-notification token for each signed-in device, used solely to deliver feed reminders.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (unless you add a baby photo), advertising identifiers, or analytics profiles, and the app contains no advertising.

How we use it

  • To sync the shared feeding record between the caregivers in your household in real time.
  • To send feed reminders and household notifications you enable.
  • To show trends, streaks, and gentle non-medical observations inside the app. These computations run on your data for your household only.

Where it lives

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, and Cloud Messaging) in the United States, protected by security rules that limit access to the members of your household. Subscription status is processed by RevenueCat and Apple; they receive purchase information, never your feeding records.

Who can see it

Only the caregivers in your household (up to five, joined by your invite code). We never sell personal information and never share it with advertisers. Service providers (Google Firebase, RevenueCat, Apple) process data on our behalf under their own privacy commitments.

Your baby's information

Livy is an app for adult caregivers. Information about your baby is entered by you, belongs to your household, and is used only to provide the app's features. It is never used for advertising or profiling.

Deleting your data

Open Care → Mascot & settings → Delete account. Deleting your account removes your sign-in and your caregiver seat immediately. If you are the household's account holder, the entire household record — every feed, streak, badge, and journal entry — is permanently deleted for all caregivers. You can also email us at the address below to request deletion.

Medical disclaimer

Livy organizes your own notes and shares general information only. Nothing in the app is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your pediatrician about your baby's health.
